Scenario — Namma Metro Smart-Card Fare System
A city metro rail divides its network into three zones – Inner, Middle and Outer. The fare is Rs 10, Rs
20 or Rs 30 according to the farthest zone travelled, with a 20% surcharge during peak hours (8–10
AM and 5–8 PM). Passengers use a stored-value smart card, and a journey is allowed only if at least
Rs 50 remains on the card after the fare is deducted. Students and senior citizens receive a
concession.
The first version of the gate software was written in a hurry. Some fares are off by a paisa, the peak
check accepts 10 AM as peak and 8 PM as off-peak inconsistently, a negative balance once appeared
on a card, and the fare values are typed in several places.
The number of journeys processed may grow:
1 station (20,000 journeys a day) → 1 line (500,000 a day) → whole network (3,000,000 a day)
You are required to analyse the problem, identify its objects, choose suitable C++ data types,
constants, operators and control structures, implement the fare logic and validate it, so that every
fare and balance is exact.
Design Challenge:
The metro is adding a fourth zone, a weekend discount and monthly passes.
Re-evaluate your original design and determine whether it should be retained, modified or
redesigned.
